The Honest Version

Most of you couldn’t hire us. That was the problem.

The system was never the bottleneck. Access was.

So we took the whole thing — the training, the vendor list, the creative playbooks, the ad account structures, the retention flows — and packaged it. Brand Scale is what we hand a client on day one of a retainer. Black Friday is the only week it’s priced for founders who aren’t on one.
